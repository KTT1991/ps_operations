# 📖 System User Guide

Welcome to the **Operations & Asset Management System** comprehensive user guide. This platform streamlines offshore and onshore project execution, asset lifecycles, mobilizing inventory, crew allocation, and offshore training matrix compliance.

---

## 1. Executive Dashboard
The **Dashboard** is the centralized operational cockpit upon login, delivering critical real-time KPIs across all departments:
- **Project Pipeline:** Quick visibility into active, mobilization-phase, and upcoming field campaigns.
- **Asset Status Distribution:** Real-time inventory status breakdown across *Available*, *In Use*, *Under Maintenance*, and *Decommissioned*.
- **Project Readiness Index:** Predictive mobilization readiness scoring evaluating required equipment and manpower certification.

---

## 2. Projects & Timeline Gantt
Visualize project schedules, milestones, and mobilization phases across an interactive Gantt chart timeline:
- **Schedule Monitoring:** Each Gantt bar displays scheduled start, end, and offshore execution duration.
- **Mobilization Phases:** Inner nested bars indicate pre-departure staging and equipment prep windows.
- **Status Indicators:**
  - **Active (Green):** Currently live on-site or offshore.
  - **Mobilization (Blue):** Preparing crew, equipment, and logistics.
  - **Delayed (Red):** Requiring operational intervention or schedule adjustment.

---

## 3. Asset & Equipment Inventory
Comprehensive lifecycle tracking for all certified tools, machinery, and instrumentation:
- **Master Asset Directory:** Central database tracking unique `Asset No`, description, equipment category, calibration expiry, and home depot.
- **Maintenance & Certification:** Preventive maintenance logging, third-party inspection certificates, and condition tracking.
- **Bulk Operations:** Download standard templates, export full inventory lists, and perform bulk batch imports.

---

## 4. Equipment Loading (Load In / Load Out)
Accurate chain of custody and physical custody tracking between base depots and offshore job sites:
- **Load In:** Records movement of assets from warehouse or depot to project sites. Automatically updates the asset's current location to the designated offshore platform or barge.
- **Load Out:** Records return of assets back to central base or depot upon campaign completion. Resets location to *Depot* and flags necessary post-job inspections.
- **Manifest Documentation:** Generate shipping manifests and cargo packing lists with verifiable tracking numbers.

---

## 5. Training & Offshore Compliance Matrix
Integrated safety and technical competency matrix tailored for energy and offshore standards (OPITO, BOSIET, H2S, First Aid, etc.):
- **Training Dashboard:** Visual compliance gauges, expiring certificate alerts (within 30/60/90 days), and department audit scores.
- **Training Matrix:** Cross-matrix matrix mapping required mandatory safety courses to job positions, displaying current compliance flags (*Compliant*, *Expiring*, *Expired*, *Missing*).
- **Training Log:** Detailed record of every completed certification with provider, completion date, certificate number, and expiry date.
- **Employee Safety Passport:** One-click comprehensive safety resume and certification passport export for client submission.
- **Excel Batch Import:** Standardized Excel template with duplicate detection and automated personnel auto-registration.

---

## 6. Resource Explorer & Advanced Search
Universal multi-entity search engine connecting Assets, Personnel, and Project records:
- **Asset Explorer:** Search any serial or asset number to inspect location history, current assignment, and past project allocations.
- **Manpower Roster & History:** Search crew by name or position to view deployment history and current status.
- **Project Snapshot:** Real-time consolidated view of all equipment, tooling, and crew members currently mobilized to a specific project.
